ABOUT IGNIS

IGNIS is an explorable timeline of NASA’s microgravity combustion and spacecraft-fire research. Each node on the home page is one experiment. Click a node and the details open below it.

WHERE THE DATA COMES FROM

Only two kinds of sources are used: (1) the NASA Physical Sciences Informatics (PSI) experiment tables supplied by the team, marked PSI, and (2) official NASA sources such as NASA pages and NASA Technical Reports Server papers, marked NASA. Where a fact could not be found it is left out, not guessed. Some PSI tables have small typing errors (for example a date typed 2103); these are flagged on the experiment.

The timeline is a curated set, not every NASA experiment. Browse the full records at the PSI repository: https://psi.nasa.gov/physci/repo/search?q=&data_source=psi&data_type=investigation. Each experiment page links to a PSI search for its own ID (for example PSI-100 for Saffire-III). Learn more on the NASA PSI history and overview page and in the PSI user guide (PDF). PSI help: msfc-psi-help@mail.nasa.gov.

NASA images and videos cannot be shown inside this page, so each experiment links to the official NASA pages that hold them.

OUR IMPACT

NASA’s flame research is spread across papers, tables and mission pages. IGNIS puts it on one timeline, from Skylab to the final Saffire flight, so students and researchers can see how the science progressed and where to find the original data.

Every fact on the site points to its NASA or PSI source.
