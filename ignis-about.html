About IGNIS

Fire behaves differently in microgravity. Without the familiar pull of Earth’s gravity, flames transform in fascinating ways, revealing combustion phenomena that help us understand fire both in space and on Earth.

IGNIS brings together real NASA experimental datasets, scientific research, interactive visualizations, and computational analysis to explore how gravity, fuel properties, and environmental conditions influence flame behaviour.

One of our key objectives is to make NASA’s scattered and diverse combustion research datasets easier to discover, navigate, and understand. By bringing relevant scientific information together in one accessible platform, IGNIS reduces the difficulty of finding and interpreting data that might otherwise stay spread across multiple sources.

Our goal is to make complex combustion science accessible while highlighting its potential for spacecraft fire safety, improved fire detection and suppression systems, and a deeper understanding of combustion on Earth.
